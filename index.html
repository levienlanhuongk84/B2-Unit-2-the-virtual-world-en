Aims of the lesson

After the lesson, you will be able to:

📚Knowledge
  • use the words and chunks of 4 groups: games, reviews, verb + noun collocations, computers and the internet;
  • form and spell ‑ly adverbs, and tell apart hard / hardly and late / lately;
  • choose the present simple or the present continuous, and keep stative verbs out of the continuous;
  • build word families with prefixes and suffixes (access → accessible → inaccessible).
🛠️Skills
  • skim and scan B2 texts for the main idea, details, reference and inference;
  • listen to messages, a conversation and a talk for key information;
  • answer VSTEP Speaking Parts 1–3 about games, the internet and technology at work;
  • write an informal email that congratulates and advises (Task 1) and an essay that discusses both views (Task 2).
💡Attitude
  • judge games and apps fairly, with reasons, not stereotypes;
  • use online health information critically and help patients find reliable sources;
  • use only English during speaking activities.

Grammar & sentence patterns

Grammar 1 · ‑ly adverbs — spelling and meaning

Four rules
➕ADJECTIVE + ‑LY
  • most adverbs = adjective + ‑ly
  • adjectives ending in ‑ll just add ‑y
  • quick → quickly · endless → endlessly · mindless → mindlessly
  • full → fully
  • The little guy jogs along endlessly.
✏️SPELLING CHANGES
  • ‑y → ‑ily
  • ‑le → ‑ly
  • ‑ic → ‑ically (but public → publicly)
  • ‑ue → ‑uly
  • easy → easily
  • remarkable → remarkably · gentle → gently
  • graphic → graphically · automatic → automatically
  • true → truly
⚡NO ‑LY: FAST · HARD · LATE · WELL
  • some adverbs have the same form as the adjective
  • the adverb of good is well
  • She types fast. · He works hard.
  • We arrived late for the film.
  • She plays chess very well.
⚠️HARDLY · LATELY
  • hardly = almost not (not ‘in a hard way’)
  • lately = recently (not ‘late’)
  • She hardly thought about work on holiday.
  • There have been a lot of good films lately.
Spelling traps
  • unfortunatelly → unfortunately · definitly → definitely
  • automaticly → automatically · immediatly → immediately
  • realy → really · especialy → especially
  • extremily → extremely · completly → completely

Grammar 2 · Present simple or present continuous?

Six rules
🔁PRESENT SIMPLE · PERMANENT AND HABITUAL
  • things that are always or generally true
  • habits and repeated actions
  • All the planets in our solar system orbit the sun.
  • I check my emails twice a day.
⏱️PRESENT SIMPLE · TIME CLAUSES AND ZERO CONDITIONAL
  • after once · as soon as · when · until about the future
  • if + present, present = what always happens
  • Once you decide where to go, let me know.
  • If you finish all the levels within an hour, you get extra points.
